Questions

Does SkyOS store my chats?

No, just short notes, which you can see and delete on your dashboard. It briefly keeps a chat’s last few messages so it understands what you mean. The details are in Privacy.

Who can read my notes?

You, and SkyOS’s operator if needed, for example when you ask for help. OpenAI processes them to write notes and doesn’t train on them. More in Privacy.

What does it cost?

Free during the beta, up to 1,000 saved messages a month. Claude and Codex work on free plans; ChatGPT needs a paid plan.
