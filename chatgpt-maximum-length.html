ChatGPT says “You’ve reached the maximum length for this conversation”

It goes on “but you can keep talking by starting a new chat.” Here’s how to bring your work with you.

Updated 18 October 2026

In ChatGPT

Sources: memory, projects.

  1. 1

    Turn on chat history

    Settings → Personalization → Memory → Reference chat history. A new chat can then find details from old ones when you ask.

  2. 2

    Paste what matters

    Memory doesn’t keep every detail, so copy the key numbers and decisions into the new chat.

  3. 3

    Keep long work in a project

    Chats in the same project can draw on each other.

Or with SkyOS

In a new chat, say “continue” and the memory’s name from your Memory page. ChatGPT gets its notes and the last few messages.

SkyOS saves as you chat, so connect it before your next long one. It works on ChatGPT’s free plan too.
