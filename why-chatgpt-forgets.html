Why does ChatGPT forget things in long chats?

A chat can only hold so much at once. Here’s why early details slip, and how to keep them.

Updated 18 October 2026

In ChatGPT

Sources: plans, memory, projects.

  1. 1

    Know how much a chat holds

    On the Free plan, ChatGPT takes in about 12 pages of your text at a time, and paid plans hold more. A longer chat no longer fits at once, so early details can be left out.

  2. 2

    Repeat what matters

    Paste the key numbers and decisions again, or ask for a summary and carry on in a new chat.

  3. 3

    Save lasting facts to memory

    Say “Remember that…” to keep a detail as a saved memory.

  4. 4

    Keep long work in a project

    Chats in the same project can draw on each other.
