Privacy Policy

SkyOS · Last updated 27 September 2026

SkyOS gives the AI tools you already use — Claude, ChatGPT and Codex — one shared memory. To do that it stores notes about your work, and a few of your recent messages, in a form our operator can read. This page says exactly what, where and for how long, so you can decide what to tell your AI tools with SkyOS connected.

Who we are

SkyOS, at skyos.ink, app.skyos.ink and api.skyos.ink, is operated by Gennadii Kosaryntsev, an individual developer based in Kazakhstan. For anything in this policy — a copy of your data, a correction, deletion, a complaint — write to hello@skyos.ink.

What SkyOS receives

SkyOS sees only what a connected AI tool sends it through SkyOS’s three tools:

  • Recall: your latest message and an ID the AI tool chose for the chat, so SkyOS can return that chat’s notes. The message is used to answer and not kept.
  • Save: your message and the AI’s answer.
  • Continue: the name of the memory you asked for.

SkyOS does not see the rest of your history in those tools, your other chats, or files you give them. What your AI tool sends is decided by the tool and the instructions you give it; each tool’s own privacy policy covers everything else it does.

What we store

  • Your account: the user ID from our sign-in provider and your email address.
  • Memories and notes: each memory’s name, and its notes — short sentences with a topic and the dates they were saved or changed.
  • Recent messages: the last five exchanges (your message and the AI’s answer) of each chat, which SkyOS reads to write notes.
  • Chats: for each chat, which tool it is in, the ID the tool gave it, which memory it uses, and when it was last used.
  • Tools: which of your tools have connected, and when each last reached SkyOS or saved.
  • Dashboard sign-ins: a record that keeps you signed in to app.skyos.ink.
  • Contact messages: what you write in the Contact form on skyos.ink, and your email address.

We don’t sell your data, show you ads, or use what you store to train AI models.

It is not end-to-end encrypted

Everything travels over encrypted connections, but it is stored in a form our operator can read. A person looks at your data only when you ask for help, to fix something that broke, to deal with abuse, or when the law requires it.

Signing in

You sign in through WorkOS, with your email address or your Google account; we never see your password. When you connect an AI tool, it signs in the same way and receives a token that lets it use SkyOS as you. The tool keeps that token; SkyOS checks it on every request and does not store it. To cut a tool off, remove SkyOS in that tool — disconnecting it on the SkyOS dashboard only updates the dashboard.

Server logs

Our logs record that something happened — a recall, a save, a sign-in, an error — with the tool’s name and a shortened fingerprint of the IDs involved. They never contain your messages, notes, email address or sign-in tokens. Vercel keeps them for a short time.

Cookies and browser storage

  • app.skyos.ink: one cookie, skyos_session, keeps you signed in to the dashboard for 30 days; skyos_login lasts an hour and exists only while you sign in. WorkOS’s sign-in pages set their own cookies. No tracking or advertising cookies.
  • skyos.ink: no cookies. Visits are counted without them, and the Contact form remembers the email address you typed in your own browser, so you don’t retype it.

How long we keep it

  • Notes and memories: until you delete them, or your account.
  • Recent messages: deleted automatically once a chat has been quiet for 30 days; only the last five are ever kept.
  • Chats that never saved anything: removed after 30 days.
  • Your account: until you ask us to delete it.
  • Dashboard sign-ins: 30 days from your last visit.
  • Contact messages: in our inbox, and a backup of the latest 500 on the server, deleted a year after the last one arrives.

Deleting your data

On the Memory page you can delete any note, or a whole memory with all its notes and the recent messages of its chats; it is gone at once. To delete your account and everything in it, write to hello@skyos.ink from the email address on your account, and we will do it within 30 days.

If you are in the EU, the EEA or the UK

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for, and, for security, abuse prevention and answering Contact messages, because we have a legitimate interest in doing so. You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, its deletion, or a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing. You can also complain to your data protection authority. Your data is processed in the United States, under the safeguards our providers offer for such transfers, such as the EU’s standard contractual clauses.

If you are in California

We do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising. You can ask to know, correct or delete what we hold about you, and we won’t treat you differently for asking.

Security

Each account’s data is kept under its own keys, and every request is checked against the account it signed in as, so one account cannot read or change another’s. Access to the production systems is limited to the operator.

Children

SkyOS is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has signed up, write to us and we will delete the account.

Changes

When this policy changes we update this page and its date. If a change matters to how your data is used, we will email you before it takes effect.
